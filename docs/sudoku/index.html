1. Overview

Sudoku is a classic Sudoku puzzle game. Puzzles are generated and graded on your device. You can play offline, keep a daily challenge streak, use teaching hints, and choose themes. There is no account, no purchases, and no personal profile data collected by the app itself. There is no custom backend and no cloud sync.

The app is free and shows ads through Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK): a banner, an interstitial and optional rewarded ads. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland, the app asks for consent on first launch through Google's consent form (User Messaging Platform). Ads are requested only after that consent allows ad requests. Personalized ads are used only if you consent; if you decline, ads are limited or non-personalized. Outside those regions, ads follow Google's default settings for that region, and the consent form may not be shown. If the device is offline at launch, the app uses the previously saved consent choice. Ads may use the advertising ID, device info and approximate location, depending on consent. Your puzzle progress, settings and stats are never sent to the ad network.

App identifier: Android package com.deredo.sudoku.

What we collect

  • Game progress, daily challenge history, stats, settings (theme, sound, music, highlights and similar), and simple ad counters (session count, interstitial and rewarded caps, ad-free pass timers), stored only on your device
  • Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) may use the advertising ID, device info and approximate location, depending on consent, along with app interaction and ad performance data and your consent status

What we don't do

  • No account, and no personal profile data collected by the app itself
  • No sending of your puzzles, notes, stats or settings to us or to the ad network
  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDK of our own
  • No purchases or subscriptions, and no selling of your data
  • No camera, microphone, photos, contacts or location permission from the app itself

3. Third-party services and SDKs

The app is built with open-source libraries (Expo, React Native and related packages such as expo-sqlite, expo-audio, expo-haptics, expo-store-review and expo-web-browser). These libraries run inside the app on your device and are not configured to send your puzzle or settings data to us. The one third-party service that receives data is Google's advertising SDK.

Google AdMob and the User Messaging Platform (UMP): the app shows ads through Google AdMob using the Google Mobile Ads SDK (react-native-google-mobile-ads):

  • A banner on the home and level-select screens
  • An interstitial after some finished puzzles (never in the first session, never mid-puzzle, with daily and time gaps)
  • Optional rewarded ads you choose to watch (extra hint, another chance after three mistakes, unlocking a missed daily challenge, or a 1-hour ad-free pass that can stack up to 3 hours)

The SDK's measurement start-up is delayed until the app initializes it. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland, Google's UMP SDK asks for consent on first launch through Google's consent form. Ads are requested only after that consent allows ad requests. Personalized ads are used only if you consent; if you decline, ads are limited or non-personalized. Outside those regions, ads follow Google's default settings for that region, and the consent form may not be shown. If the device is offline at launch, the app uses the previously saved consent choice. The app does not pass board contents, notes, stats or settings to Google, and it does not collect an account, purchases or personal profile data of its own.

When it serves ads, and depending on consent, Google may use the advertising ID (the Android advertising ID and app set ID), device info (such as device model, operating system and SDK performance) and approximate location derived from your IP address, along with app interaction and ad performance data (such as ad impressions and taps) and your consent status. Google uses this data to deliver and measure ads, cap how often ads appear, and detect fraud. Learn more in How Google uses data in advertising, How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

In-app review: the app may ask you to rate it using Google Play's in-app review API on the device. That flow is handled by Google Play under Google's terms. The app does not send your puzzle data with that request.

Apart from ad and store-review network use, the app is designed to play fully offline. Puzzle generation and solving run on the device.

4. Device permissions

Sudoku does not ask for camera, microphone, photos, contacts, notification or fine location permissions. Background music and sound effects use bundled audio files; microphone recording is disabled in the app's configuration.

5. Data retention

Game progress, daily history, stats, settings and ad counters stay on your device until you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.

Data collected by Google through the ads SDK is held by Google and retained under Google's own terms and policies (see the Google Privacy Policy). We do not receive it in a form that identifies you and cannot delete it for you.

Your device's operating system may include Sudoku's data in device backups that you control (for example, Google device backup on Android). Those backups are handled by Google under your account and its privacy policy. We have no access to them.

6. Data security

Your data stays in the app's private storage area on your device, which the operating system protects from other apps. Its protection also depends on your device's security, such as your screen lock and device encryption. We recommend you keep your device locked and up to date. Your puzzles and settings are never sent over the network by the app. Google states that data collected by its ads SDK is encrypted in transit.

7. Your rights and choices

Because Sudoku keeps your game data only on your device, you control it directly and we hold no copy of it. Depending on where you live, laws such as the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) or the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA) give you rights to access, correct, delete or port your personal data, to object to or restrict processing, and to withdraw consent. In this app:

  • Access and correction: the data the app stores is shown in the app, and you can change settings and continue or restart games where the app allows it.
  • Deletion: clear the app's data or uninstall the app (see section 8).
  • Ad consent: in the EEA, UK and Switzerland, Google's consent form is shown on first launch. Personalized ads are used only if you consent; if you decline, ads are limited or non-personalized. You can change or withdraw your consent at any time in Settings → Privacy options (where that entry is shown). You can also uninstall the app, or reset the advertising ID in Android Settings → Google → Ads.
  • Advertising ID: on Android, go to Settings → Privacy → Ads (or Settings → Google → Ads) to reset or delete your advertising ID.
  • Rewarded ads are optional: you never have to watch one to play; free hints are limited per game, and other rewards are opt-in.
  • No sale: we do not sell your personal information.

If you have a question about your rights, contact us at the address below. Because we receive no data from the app, we cannot look up or recover data stored on your device. For data held by Google, see Google's privacy controls.

8. How to delete your data

Sudoku has no account, so there is no account to delete. To delete your data:

  1. Clear storage: on Android, open Settings → Apps → Sudoku → Storage → Clear storage. This deletes games, stats, settings and ad counters while keeping the app installed.
  2. Uninstall: uninstall Sudoku to remove the app and its data from the device.
  3. Ad data: reset or delete your advertising ID as described in section 7. Data already collected by Google is handled under Google's terms.

9. Children's privacy

Sudoku is not directed at children under 13 (or under the age of digital consent where you live), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. The app shows ads and is not part of any family or children's program. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will help remove it.

Sudoku shows ads through Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK). In the EEA, UK and Switzerland, the app asks for consent on first launch through Google's consent form. Ads are requested only after that consent allows ad requests. Personalized ads are used only if you consent; if you decline, ads are limited or non-personalized. Outside those regions, ads follow Google's default settings for that region, and the consent form may not be shown. If the device is offline at launch, the app uses the previously saved consent choice. Ads may use the advertising ID, device info and approximate location, depending on consent. Tracking here means linking data collected in this app with data from other companies' apps, websites or offline properties for targeted advertising or advertising measurement, or sharing it with data brokers. The app does not implement its own cross-app tracking, and it collects no account, no purchases and no personal profile data of its own. You can change or withdraw consent in Settings → Privacy options where that entry is shown. Even when ads are limited by consent or device settings, Google may still access the advertising ID (where your device settings allow it), device info, approximate location from your IP address and the other data listed in section 3 for ad delivery, frequency capping, aggregated measurement and fraud prevention. Your puzzles, notes, stats and settings are never shared with Google or any ad network. See how Google uses data in advertising.
