1. Overview

Bloom is a habit tracker. You create habits, mark them done, and watch a garden-style calendar grow. Habits, completions and settings stay on your device. There is no account and no remote server, and we never receive your habit data.

What we collect

  • Habits you create (title, description, schedule, reminder time, category, color), stored only on your device
  • Completion history (which habit on which date), stored only on your device
  • Appearance and notification settings, stored only on your device

What we don't do

  • No account or personal details
  • No upload of your habits to us or anyone else
  • No analytics, ads, crash reporting or tracking
  • No push notifications or push tokens
  • No selling or sharing of data

3. Third-party services and SDKs

The app is built with open-source libraries (Expo, React Native and related packages). These libraries run inside the app on your device. In the code of this version, none of them is configured to send your data to us or to any third party. The app contains no analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking SDKs.

Reminders are local notifications. The app schedules them on your device. They are not push notifications. The app does not register a push token, and no server is involved.

Reminder notifications show the habit's title and description on your lock screen. Leave the description empty if you would rather not show it there.

4. Device permissions

The app asks for a permission only when you use a feature that needs it. You can grant or revoke permissions at any time in your device settings. Denying a permission only disables the related feature.

5. Data retention

Your data stays on your device until you delete it in the app or uninstall the app. We keep no copies.

Your device's operating system may include Bloom's data in device backups that you control (for example, iCloud Backup on iOS or Google device backup on Android). Those backups are handled by Apple or Google under your account and their privacy policies (Apple Privacy Policy, Google Privacy Policy). We have no access to them.

6. Data security

Your data stays in the app's private storage area on your device, which the operating system protects from other apps. Its protection also depends on your device's security, such as your screen lock and device encryption. We recommend you keep your device locked and up to date. Because the app does not send this data over the network, no data is sent in transit.

7. Your rights and choices

Because Bloom keeps your data only on your device, you control it directly and we hold no copy of it. Depending on where you live, laws such as the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) or the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA) give you rights to access, correct, delete or port your personal data, to object to or restrict processing, and to withdraw consent. In this app:

  • Access and correction: the data the app stores is shown in the app, and you can edit it wherever the app allows editing.
  • Deletion: delete single habits, or use Settings → Reset habit data. Uninstalling removes everything.
  • Withdrawing consent: you can revoke any device permission at any time in your device settings.
  • No sale or sharing: we do not sell your personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

If you have a question about your rights, contact us at the address below. Because we receive no data from the app, we cannot look up or recover data stored on your device.

8. How to delete your data

Bloom has no account, so there is no account to delete. To delete your data:

  1. Delete one habit: open the habit's edit form and delete it. Its history is also removed.
  2. Delete all habits and history: open the Settings tab → Reset data → confirm Reset. Your appearance and notification settings are kept.
  3. Remove everything: uninstall Bloom. On Android you can also use Settings → Apps → Bloom → Storage → Clear storage.

9. Children's privacy

Bloom is a general-audience app. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or under the age of digital consent where you live). If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will help remove it.

10. Tracking and advertising

Bloom does not track you. As Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework defines it, tracking means linking data collected in this app with data from other companies' apps, websites or offline properties for targeted advertising or advertising measurement, or sharing it with data brokers. Bloom does none of that. It shows no ads, contains no advertising identifiers or analytics SDKs, and does not ask for tracking permission.

If a change materially affects how we handle your personal data, we will give notice in the app or by other reasonable means before it takes effect. Please review this page from time to time.
