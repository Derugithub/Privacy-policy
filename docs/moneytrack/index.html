1. Overview

MoneyTrack is an expense tracker and budget planner. You set monthly category limits, log income and expenses, and see what is left in your plan. Your entries, budgets and settings stay on your device. There is no account and no server of ours, and we never receive your financial information.

The app is free and shows ads from Google AdMob, starting from your second session (never during your first session). In the EEA, UK and Switzerland, the app asks for consent on first launch through Google's consent form. Ads are requested only after that consent allows ad requests. Personalized ads are used only if you consent; if you decline, ads are limited or non-personalized. Outside those regions, ads follow Google's default settings for that region, and the consent form may not be shown. If the device is offline at launch, the app uses the previously saved consent choice. Your entries, amounts, categories, notes and budgets are never sent to the ad network.

App identifier: Android package com.deredo.moneytrack.

What we collect

  • Income and expense entries you type in (amount, category, date, optional note), budgets, custom categories and your chosen currency, stored only on your device
  • Your theme choice and simple ad counters (session count, daily interstitial and ad-free pass counts, ad timers), stored only on your device
  • Google AdMob (from session 2) and Google's consent tool collect the advertising ID, approximate location derived from your IP address, app interaction and ad performance data, device and diagnostic information, and your consent status

What we don't do

  • No account, bank connection or card details
  • No sending of your entries, budgets or notes to us or to the ad network
  • No personalized (targeted) ads in the EEA, UK or Switzerland unless you allow them in Google's consent form; if you decline, ads there are limited or non-personalized. Outside those regions, ads follow Google's default settings for that region
  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDK of our own
  • No payments or in-app purchases, and no selling of your data

3. Third-party services and SDKs

The app is built with open-source libraries (Expo, React Native and related packages). These libraries run inside the app on your device and are not configured to send your data to us. Fonts are bundled inside the app, so no font requests are made. The one third-party service that receives data is Google's advertising SDK, together with its consent tool.

Google User Messaging Platform (UMP): every time the app starts, including your first session, Google's UMP SDK checks whether consent is needed. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland, the app asks for consent on first launch through Google's consent form. No ad is requested, and the ads SDK is not started, until that consent status allows ad requests. Personalized ads are used only if you consent; if you decline, ads are limited or non-personalized. Outside those regions, ads follow Google's default settings for that region, and the consent form may not be shown. If the device is offline at launch, the app uses the previously saved consent choice.

Google AdMob: from your second session, the app uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (react-native-google-mobile-ads) to show:

  • a banner at the bottom of the main screen, labelled "Ad" and kept apart from your totals. It is hidden while you type, while a dialog or editor is open, and during ad-free time;
  • from your third session, an interstitial (full-screen) ad after every fourth entry you save in a session, at most 8 per day and at least 4 minutes apart, never after the first entry of a session or right after you open the app;
  • optional rewarded ads that you choose to watch for 1 hour without banner and interstitial ads (up to 6 per day, adding up to at most 3 hours).

The app does not pass your entries, amounts, categories, notes, budgets or currency to Google.

When it serves ads, Google may collect: the device advertising identifier (the Android advertising ID and app set ID), approximate location derived from your IP address, app interaction and ad performance data (such as ad impressions and taps), device and diagnostic information (such as device model, operating system and SDK performance), and your consent status. Google uses this data to deliver and measure ads, cap how often ads appear, and detect fraud, including when ads are limited or non-personalized. Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services, How Google uses cookies and identifiers in advertising and the Google Privacy Policy.

5. Network use

The app makes only these network requests:

  • Consent and ads: requests from your device to Google, as described in section 3.
  • Ad settings file (optional): builds that are configured with an ad settings address download a small settings file from that HTTPS address when the app starts. The request is a plain download that sends no personal data and none of your entries. Like any web request, it reveals your IP address and basic request details (such as the app's network user agent) to the server that hosts the file. The file only says which ad formats are on and how often interstitials may appear. If it cannot be loaded, the app uses its built-in settings.
  • Contact support: tapping Contact support opens your email app with a new message to us. Nothing is sent unless you send that email.

6. Data retention

Your entries, budgets and categories stay on your device until you remove them, use Clear data, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app. Clear data erases your transactions, budgets and custom categories, but keeps your currency and theme choices and the ad counters, so it does not reset how often ads appear or your remaining ad-free time. Ad counters, the cached ad settings file and preferences stay on your device until you clear the app's storage or uninstall the app. We keep no copies.

Data collected by Google through the ads SDK and consent tool is held by Google and retained under Google's own terms and policies (see the Google Privacy Policy). We do not receive it in a form that identifies you and cannot delete it for you.

Your device's operating system may include MoneyTrack's data in device backups that you control (for example, Google device backup on Android). Those backups are handled by Google under your account and its privacy policy (Google Privacy Policy). We have no access to them.

7. Data security

Your data stays in the app's private storage area on your device, which the operating system protects from other apps. Its protection also depends on your device's security, such as your screen lock and device encryption. We recommend you keep your device locked and up to date. Your entries are never sent over the network by the app. The optional ad settings file is only downloaded over HTTPS. Google states that data collected by its ads SDK is encrypted in transit.

8. Your rights and choices

Because MoneyTrack keeps your data only on your device, you control it directly and we hold no copy of it. Depending on where you live, laws such as the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) or the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA) give you rights to access, correct, delete or port your personal data, to object to or restrict processing, and to withdraw consent. In this app:

  • Access and correction: the data the app stores is shown in the app, and you can edit entries and limits in the app.
  • Deletion: remove single entries, use Clear data, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app (see section 9).
  • Ad consent: in the EEA, UK and Switzerland, Google's consent form is shown on first launch, before ads are requested. You can change or withdraw your consent at any time in the app, using Privacy options at the bottom of the month panel, next to Contact support and Clear data (where that entry is shown). You can also uninstall the app, or reset the advertising ID in Android Settings → Google → Ads.
  • Advertising ID: on Android, go to Settings → Privacy → Ads (or Settings → Google → Ads) to reset or delete your advertising ID.
  • Rewarded ads are optional: you never have to watch one to use the app.
  • Withdrawing consent: you can revoke any device permission at any time in your device settings.
  • No sale: we do not sell your personal information. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland, ads are personalized only if you allow that in Google's consent form; if you decline, they are limited or non-personalized. Outside those regions, ads follow Google's default settings for that region.

If you have a question about your rights, contact us at the address below. Because we receive no data from the app, we cannot look up or recover data stored on your device. For data held by Google, see Google's privacy controls.

9. How to delete your data

MoneyTrack has no account, so there is no account to delete. To delete your data:

  1. Remove one entry: tap Remove next to the entry in the ledger, or open the entry and tap Remove.
  2. Erase your ledger: tap Clear data at the bottom of the month panel → confirm Erase. All transactions, budgets and custom categories are removed from the device. Your currency and theme choices and the ad counters are kept.
  3. Remove everything: uninstall MoneyTrack. On Android you can also go to Settings → Apps → MoneyTrack → Storage → Clear storage. This also deletes the ad counters, the cached ad settings and your preferences.
  4. Ad data: reset or delete your advertising ID as described in section 8. Data already collected by Google is handled under Google's terms.

10. Children's privacy

MoneyTrack is not directed at children under 13 (or under the age of digital consent where you live), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. The app shows ads and is not part of any family or children's program. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will help remove it.

MoneyTrack shows ads from Google AdMob. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland, the app asks for consent on first launch through Google's consent form. Ads are requested only after that consent allows ad requests. Personalized ads are used only if you consent; if you decline, ads are limited or non-personalized. Outside those regions, ads follow Google's default settings for that region, and the consent form may not be shown. If the device is offline at launch, the app uses the previously saved consent choice. Tracking here means linking data collected in this app with data from other companies' apps, websites or offline properties for targeted advertising or advertising measurement, or sharing it with data brokers. MoneyTrack does not implement its own cross-app tracking. You can change or withdraw consent in the app, using Privacy options at the bottom of the month panel, where that entry is shown. Even when ads are limited or non-personalized, Google may still access the device advertising identifier (where your device settings allow it), your IP address and the other data listed in section 3 for ad delivery, frequency capping, aggregated measurement and fraud prevention. Your entries, amounts, categories, notes and budgets are never shared with Google or any ad network.
