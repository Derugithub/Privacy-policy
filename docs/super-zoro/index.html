1. Overview

Super Zoro is a 2D platformer game. You run and jump through 40 levels, collect coins and spend them in an in-game shop. Your progress (unlocked levels, stars, best times, coins, shop items and sound settings) is saved on your device. There is no account, no sign-in, no custom backend and no cloud sync, and we never receive your game data.

The game is free and shows ads through Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK): a banner on the title screen, the world map and the shop, a full-screen (interstitial) ad that can appear on the results screen after some level clears, and optional rewarded ads that you choose to watch in the shop to get an item. Ads are not shown while a level is being played. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland, the game asks for consent on first launch through Google's consent form (User Messaging Platform). Ads are requested only after that consent allows ad requests. Personalized ads are used only if you consent; if you decline, ads are limited or non-personalized. Outside those regions, ads follow Google's default settings for that region, and the consent form may not be shown. If the device is offline at launch, the game uses the previously saved consent choice and plays normally without ads. Ads may use the advertising ID, device info and approximate location, depending on consent. Your game progress is never sent to the ad network.

App identifier: Android package com.superzoro.game.

What we collect

  • Game progress (unlocked levels, stars, best times, coins collected per level), your coin wallet, shop items you own, daily tasks and music and sound settings, stored only on your device
  • Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) may use the advertising ID, device info and approximate location, depending on consent, along with app interaction and ad performance data and your consent status

What we don't do

  • No account, sign-in or personal profile data collected by the game itself
  • No sending of your progress, coins, items or settings to us or to the ad network
  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDK of our own
  • No push notifications
  • No in-app purchases or subscriptions (coins are earned in the game and cannot be bought), and no selling of your data
  • No camera, microphone, photos, contacts, location or storage permission

3. Third-party services and SDKs

The game is built with the open-source Godot Engine and the Poing Studios Godot AdMob plugin, which connects the game to Google's advertising SDK. The engine runs inside the app on your device and is not configured to send your game data to us. The one third-party service that receives data is Google's advertising SDK.

Google AdMob and the User Messaging Platform (UMP): the game shows ads through Google AdMob using the Google Mobile Ads SDK: a banner on the title screen, world map and shop, an occasional full-screen ad on the results screen after level clears, and rewarded ads that you choose to watch in the shop. A rewarded item is given only when Google reports that the ad was watched. Ads appear only after consent allows ad requests.

In the EEA, UK and Switzerland, Google's UMP SDK asks for consent on first launch through Google's consent form. Ads are requested only after that consent allows ad requests. Personalized ads are used only if you consent; if you decline, ads are limited or non-personalized. Outside those regions, ads follow Google's default settings for that region, and the consent form may not be shown. If the device is offline at launch, the game uses the previously saved consent choice. The game does not pass your progress, coins, items or settings to Google, and it does not collect an account, purchases or personal profile data of its own.

When it serves ads, and depending on consent, Google may use the advertising ID (the Android advertising ID and app set ID), device info (such as device model, operating system and SDK performance) and approximate location derived from your IP address, along with app interaction and ad performance data (such as ad impressions and taps) and your consent status. Google uses this data to deliver and measure ads, cap how often ads appear, and detect fraud. Learn more in How Google uses data in advertising, How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services, the AdMob privacy and messaging help and the Google Privacy Policy.

Privacy Policy link: the Privacy Policy button in Settings opens this page in your browser, where your browser's own privacy policy applies.

Apart from ad and consent network use, the game is designed to work fully offline.

4. Device permissions

Super Zoro does not ask you for any runtime permission. It does not use the camera, microphone, photos, contacts, location or storage. The permissions below are granted automatically at install and are used by the ads SDK.

PermissionWhy it is needed
Internet and network state (INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE, Android, automatic)Loading ads and the consent form, and telling you when you are offline.
Advertising ID (Android, com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID, automatic)Lets the Google Mobile Ads SDK read the advertising ID for ad delivery, frequency capping, measurement and fraud prevention. You can reset or delete it in Android settings.
Wake lock (WAKE_LOCK, Android, automatic)Used by the Google Mobile Ads SDK to complete short background tasks.

5. Data retention

Your game progress and settings stay on your device until you clear the app's data or uninstall the app. We keep no copies. The game turns off Android app data backup, so your save file is not copied to device backups.

Data collected by Google through the ads SDK is held by Google and retained under Google's own terms and policies (see the Google Privacy Policy). We do not receive it in a form that identifies you and cannot delete it for you.

6. Data security

Your save file stays in the app's private storage area on your device, which the operating system protects from other apps. Its protection also depends on your device's security, such as your screen lock and device encryption. We recommend you keep your device locked and up to date. Your progress and settings are never sent over the network by the game. Google states that data collected by its ads SDK is encrypted in transit.

7. Your rights and choices

Because Super Zoro keeps your game data only on your device, you control it directly and we hold no copy of it. Depending on where you live, laws such as the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) or the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA) give you rights to access, correct, delete or port your personal data, to object to or restrict processing, and to withdraw consent. In this app:

  • Access: the data the game stores is shown in the game (levels, stars, coins, items and settings).
  • Deletion: clear the app's data or uninstall the app (see section 8).
  • Ad consent: in the EEA, UK and Switzerland, Google's consent form is shown on first launch. Personalized ads are used only if you consent; if you decline, ads are limited or non-personalized. You can change or withdraw your consent at any time in Settings → Ad privacy (where that button is shown). You can also uninstall the app.
  • Rewarded ads are optional: you never have to watch a rewarded ad; items can also be bought with coins earned in the game.
  • Advertising ID: on Android, go to Settings → Privacy → Ads (or Settings → Google → Ads) to reset or delete your advertising ID.
  • No sale: we do not sell your personal information.

If you have a question about your rights, contact us at the address below. Because we receive no game data from the app, we cannot look up or recover data stored on your device. For data held by Google, see Google's privacy controls.

8. How to delete your data

Super Zoro has no account, so there is no account to delete. To delete your data:

  1. Clear everything: on Android, open Settings → Apps → Super Zoro → Storage → Clear storage. This deletes all progress, coins, items and settings while keeping the game installed.
  2. Uninstall: uninstall Super Zoro to remove the game and its data from the device.
  3. Ad data: reset or delete your advertising ID as described in section 7. Data already collected by Google is handled under Google's terms.

9. Children's privacy

Super Zoro is not directed at children under 13 (or under the age of digital consent where you live), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. The game itself collects no personal information from anyone, and it shows ads served by Google AdMob. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will help remove it.

10. Tracking and advertising

Super Zoro shows ads through Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK). In the EEA, UK and Switzerland, the game asks for consent on first launch through Google's consent form. Ads are requested only after that consent allows ad requests. Personalized ads are used only if you consent; if you decline, ads are limited or non-personalized. Outside those regions, ads follow Google's default settings for that region, and the consent form may not be shown. If the device is offline at launch, the game uses the previously saved consent choice. Ads may use the advertising ID, device info and approximate location, depending on consent. Tracking here means linking data collected in this app with data from other companies' apps, websites or offline properties for targeted advertising or advertising measurement, or sharing it with data brokers. The game does not implement its own cross-app tracking, and it collects no account, no purchases and no personal profile data of its own. You can change or withdraw consent in Settings → Ad privacy where that button is shown. Even when ads are limited by consent or device settings, Google may still access the advertising ID (where your device settings allow it), device info, approximate location from your IP address and the other data listed in section 3 for ad delivery, frequency capping, aggregated measurement and fraud prevention. Your game progress, coins, items and settings are never shared with Google or any ad network. See how Google uses data in advertising.
