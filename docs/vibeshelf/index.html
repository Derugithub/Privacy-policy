1. Overview

VibeShelf lets you build mood boards from your photos. You add photos from your library or take new ones, the app tags them by color and light on your device, and you arrange them into boards and export collages. There is no account, no server of ours and no cloud AI. Your photos, notes and boards are never uploaded to us.

The app is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland, the app asks for consent on first launch through Google's consent form. Ads are requested only after that consent allows ad requests. Personalized ads are used only if you consent; if you decline, ads are limited or non-personalized. Outside those regions, ads follow Google's default settings for that region, and the consent form may not be shown. If the device is offline at launch, the app uses the previously saved consent choice. Your photos, tags, notes and boards are never sent to the ad network.

App identifier: Android package com.deredo.vibeshelf.

What we collect

  • Copies of photos you choose to add (from your library or camera), plus small thumbnails, stored only on your device
  • Color, brightness and vibe tags computed on the device, your notes and boards, and a duplicate-check fingerprint for each photo, stored only on your device
  • Your theme and haptics choices, simple ad counters and a review-prompt counter, stored only on your device
  • Google AdMob and Google's consent tool collect the advertising ID, approximate location derived from your IP address, app interaction and ad performance data, device and diagnostic information, and your consent status

What we don't do

  • No account or personal details
  • No upload of your photos, and no cloud or AI analysis
  • No photo location or EXIF metadata read (the app asks the picker and camera for photos without EXIF data)
  • No sending of your photos, tags, notes or boards to us or to the ad network
  • No personalized (targeted) ads in the EEA, UK or Switzerland unless you allow them in Google's consent form; if you decline, ads there are limited or non-personalized. Outside those regions, ads follow Google's default settings for that region
  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDK of our own
  • No payments or in-app purchases, and no selling of your data

3. Third-party services and SDKs

The app is built with open-source libraries (Expo, React Native and related packages). These libraries run inside the app on your device and are not configured to send your data to us. Fonts are bundled inside the app, so no font requests are made. The sample photos are bundled inside the app too. The one third-party service that receives data is Google's advertising SDK, together with its consent tool.

Google User Messaging Platform (UMP): every time the app starts, Google's UMP SDK checks whether consent is needed. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland, the app asks for consent on first launch through Google's consent form. No ad is requested, and the ads SDK is not started, until that consent status allows ad requests. Personalized ads are used only if you consent; if you decline, ads are limited or non-personalized. Outside those regions, ads follow Google's default settings for that region, and the consent form may not be shown. If the device is offline at launch, the app uses the previously saved consent choice.

Google AdMob: the app uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (react-native-google-mobile-ads) to show:

  • a banner on the Library and Boards screens only, never on the photo, board, export, intro or settings screens, and hidden during ad-free time;
  • from your second session, an interstitial (full-screen) ad after every second successful collage export, shown only after the share sheet and export preview have closed, at most 3 per day and at least 3 minutes apart, never in the first minute of a session or within 3 minutes after a rewarded ad;
  • optional rewarded ads that you choose to watch in Settings → Remove ads for 1 hour, for 1 hour without banner and interstitial ads (up to 6 per day, adding up to at most 3 hours).

The app does not pass your photos, tags, colors, notes or boards to Google.

When it serves ads, Google may collect: the device advertising identifier (the Android advertising ID and app set ID), approximate location derived from your IP address, app interaction and ad performance data (such as ad impressions and taps), device and diagnostic information (such as device model, operating system and SDK performance), and your consent status. Google uses this data to deliver and measure ads, cap how often ads appear, and detect fraud, including when ads are limited or non-personalized. Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services, How Google uses cookies and identifiers in advertising and the Google Privacy Policy.

Google Play in-app review: after a few successful collage exports (from your third session), the app may ask Google Play to show its rating prompt. Google Play decides whether to show it and handles any rating you leave under Google's terms. Rate us in Settings opens the VibeShelf page in the Google Play Store app.

4. Device permissions

The app asks for a permission only when you use a feature that needs it. You can grant or revoke permissions at any time in your device settings. Denying a permission only disables the related feature, and the bundled sample photos still work. VibeShelf does not ask for microphone, location, contacts, notification or tracking permissions, and it does not request storage or media-library permissions.

5. Network use

Tagging, boards and export work offline. The app makes only these network requests:

  • Consent and ads: requests from your device to Google, as described in section 3.
  • Google Play: the in-app rating prompt and the Rate us link are handled by Google Play.
  • Sharing: a collage leaves your device only if you share it, through the app you pick in the share sheet.

6. Data retention

Photo copies, thumbnails, tags, notes, boards and fingerprints stay on your device until you remove them in the app, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app. Settings, ad counters and the review counter stay on your device until you clear the app's storage or uninstall the app. We keep no copies.

Data collected by Google through the ads SDK and consent tool is held by Google and retained under Google's own terms and policies (see the Google Privacy Policy). We do not receive it in a form that identifies you and cannot delete it for you.

Your device's operating system may include VibeShelf's data in device backups that you control (for example, Google device backup on Android). Those backups are handled by Google under your account and its privacy policy (Google Privacy Policy). We have no access to them.

7. Data security

Your data stays in the app's private storage area on your device, which the operating system protects from other apps. Its protection also depends on your device's security, such as your screen lock and device encryption. We recommend you keep your device locked and up to date. Your photos, tags, notes and boards are never sent over the network by the app. Google states that data collected by its ads SDK is encrypted in transit.

8. Your rights and choices

Because VibeShelf keeps your data only on your device, you control it directly and we hold no copy of it. Depending on where you live, laws such as the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) or the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA) give you rights to access, correct, delete or port your personal data, to object to or restrict processing, and to withdraw consent. In this app:

  • Access and correction: the data the app stores is shown in the app, and you can edit notes, boards and settings in the app.
  • Deletion: remove photos from your shelf, delete boards, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app (see section 9).
  • Ad consent: in the EEA, UK and Switzerland, Google's consent form is shown on first launch, before ads are requested. You can change or withdraw your consent at any time in the app, using Settings → Ads → Privacy options (where that entry is shown). You can also uninstall the app, or reset the advertising ID in Android Settings → Google → Ads.
  • Advertising ID: on Android, go to Settings → Privacy → Ads (or Settings → Google → Ads) to reset or delete your advertising ID.
  • Rewarded ads are optional: you never have to watch one to use the app.
  • Withdrawing consent: you can revoke any device permission at any time in your device settings.
  • No sale: we do not sell your personal information. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland, ads are personalized only if you allow that in Google's consent form; if you decline, they are limited or non-personalized. Outside those regions, ads follow Google's default settings for that region.

If you have a question about your rights, contact us at the address below. Because we receive no data from the app, we cannot look up or recover data stored on your device. For data held by Google, see Google's privacy controls.

9. How to delete your data

VibeShelf has no account, so there is no account to delete. To delete your data:

  1. Remove a photo: open the photo and tap Remove from shelf. The app's copy and thumbnail are deleted, the photo disappears from all boards, and the original in your photo library is not touched.
  2. Remove a photo from one board only: open the board and use Remove from board. The photo stays on your shelf.
  3. Delete a board: open the board and tap Delete board. Its photos stay on your shelf until you remove them.
  4. Remove everything: uninstall VibeShelf. On Android you can also go to Settings → Apps → VibeShelf → Storage → Clear storage. This also deletes your settings, the ad counters and the review counter.
  5. Collages you shared or saved: delete them where you sent them, or from your gallery.
  6. Ad data: reset or delete your advertising ID as described in section 8. Data already collected by Google is handled under Google's terms.

10. Children's privacy

VibeShelf is not directed at children under 13 (or under the age of digital consent where you live), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. The app shows ads and is not part of any family or children's program. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will help remove it.

11. Tracking and advertising

VibeShelf shows ads from Google AdMob. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland, the app asks for consent on first launch through Google's consent form. Ads are requested only after that consent allows ad requests. Personalized ads are used only if you consent; if you decline, ads are limited or non-personalized. Outside those regions, ads follow Google's default settings for that region, and the consent form may not be shown. If the device is offline at launch, the app uses the previously saved consent choice. Tracking here means linking data collected in this app with data from other companies' apps, websites or offline properties for targeted advertising or advertising measurement, or sharing it with data brokers. VibeShelf does not implement its own cross-app tracking. You can change or withdraw consent in the app, using Settings → Ads → Privacy options, where that entry is shown. Even when ads are limited or non-personalized, Google may still access the device advertising identifier (where your device settings allow it), your IP address and the other data listed in section 3 for ad delivery, frequency capping, aggregated measurement and fraud prevention. Your photos, tags, notes and boards are never shared with Google or any ad network.
