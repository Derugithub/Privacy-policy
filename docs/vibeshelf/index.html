1. Overview

VibeShelf lets you build mood boards from your photos. You import photos or take new ones, the app tags them by color and light on your device, and you arrange them into boards and export collages. There is no account, no backend and no cloud AI. Your photos are never uploaded to us.

What we collect

  • Copies of photos you choose to add (from your library or camera), stored only on your device
  • Color, brightness and vibe tags computed on the device, plus your notes and boards, stored only on your device

What we don't do

  • No account or personal details
  • No upload of photos, and no cloud or AI analysis
  • No photo location or EXIF metadata read (the app asks the picker for photos without EXIF data)
  • No analytics, ads, crash reporting or tracking
  • No selling or sharing of data

3. Third-party services and SDKs

The app is built with open-source libraries (Expo, React Native and related packages). These libraries run inside the app on your device. In the code of this version, none of them is configured to send your data to us or to any third party. The app contains no analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking SDKs.

When you export a collage, the system share sheet (provided by iOS or Android) sends the image to the app or contact you pick. That recipient's own privacy policy then applies.

4. Device permissions

The app asks for a permission only when you use a feature that needs it. You can grant or revoke permissions at any time in your device settings. Denying a permission only disables the related feature.

PermissionWhy it is needed
Photo library (iOS NSPhotoLibraryUsageDescription; Android photo/media access)Choosing photos to add to your shelf. Asked only when you tap import. Only the photos you pick are copied.

Microphone access is disabled. If you deny photo or camera access, the bundled sample photos still work.

On Android, React Native apps include the standard INTERNET permission by default. This permission does not show a prompt. This app's code does not use it to send your data anywhere.

5. Data retention

Photo copies, tags, notes and boards stay on your device until you remove them in the app or uninstall the app.

Your device's operating system may include VibeShelf's data in device backups that you control (for example, iCloud Backup on iOS or Google device backup on Android). Those backups are handled by Apple or Google under your account and their privacy policies (Apple Privacy Policy, Google Privacy Policy). We have no access to them.

6. Data security

Your data stays in the app's private storage area on your device, which the operating system protects from other apps. Its protection also depends on your device's security, such as your screen lock and device encryption. We recommend you keep your device locked and up to date. Because the app does not send this data over the network, no data is sent in transit.

7. Your rights and choices

Because VibeShelf keeps your data only on your device, you control it directly and we hold no copy of it. Depending on where you live, laws such as the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) or the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA) give you rights to access, correct, delete or port your personal data, to object to or restrict processing, and to withdraw consent. In this app:

  • Access and correction: the data the app stores is shown in the app, and you can edit it wherever the app allows editing.
  • Deletion: remove photos from your shelf and delete boards in the app, or uninstall the app.
  • Withdrawing consent: you can revoke any device permission at any time in your device settings.
  • No sale or sharing: we do not sell your personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

If you have a question about your rights, contact us at the address below. Because we receive no data from the app, we cannot look up or recover data stored on your device.

8. How to delete your data

VibeShelf has no account, so there is no account to delete. To delete your data:

  1. Remove a photo: open the photo → remove it from the shelf. The app's copy is deleted, it disappears from all boards, and the original in your photo library is not touched.
  2. Delete a board: open the Boards tab and delete the board. Its photos stay on your shelf until you remove them.
  3. Remove everything: uninstall VibeShelf. On Android you can also use Settings → Apps → VibeShelf → Storage → Clear storage.

Collages you already shared or saved elsewhere must be deleted where you sent them.

9. Children's privacy

VibeShelf is a general-audience app. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or under the age of digital consent where you live). If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will help remove it.

10. Tracking and advertising

VibeShelf does not track you. As Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework defines it, tracking means linking data collected in this app with data from other companies' apps, websites or offline properties for targeted advertising or advertising measurement, or sharing it with data brokers. VibeShelf does none of that. It shows no ads, contains no advertising identifiers or analytics SDKs, and does not ask for tracking permission.
