1. Overview

NoirList is a task list. Your tasks, categories and settings are stored on your device only. The app has no account and no server of its own, and we never receive your tasks.

The app is free and shows ads through Google AdMob. To show ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK in the app collects some device and usage data and sends it to Google, as described in section 3. Users in the European Economic Area (EEA), the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent first through Google's consent message.

App identifier: Android package com.movad.NoirList.

What is collected

  • Tasks, categories and settings you create, stored only on your device
  • For ads (collected by Google, not by us): advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interaction data
  • Your ad consent choice, stored on your device by Google's consent SDK

What we don't do

  • No account or personal details such as name or email
  • We never receive or upload your tasks
  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDKs
  • No location, contacts, camera, microphone or storage permissions
  • No selling of your tasks or personal data by us

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

NoirList shows a banner ad at the bottom of the task list, served by Google AdMob. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share the data listed above with Google, and Google may use it to provide, personalize and measure ads and to prevent fraud. Data is encrypted in transit. Google's handling of this data is described in the Google Privacy Policy, How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and How Google uses advertising.

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: on first launch the app shows Google's consent message. Ads load only after the consent SDK allows it. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy options in the app (this button appears where the consent rules apply).
  • Everywhere: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on your device). You may still see ads, but they will be less relevant to you.

6. Data retention

Tasks, categories and settings stay on your device until you delete them or uninstall the app. Data collected by Google for advertising is kept according to the Google Privacy Policy.

Your device's operating system may include NoirList's data in Android device backups that you control. Those backups are handled by Google under your account and the Google Privacy Policy.

7. Data security

Your tasks stay in the app's private storage area on your device, which Android protects from other apps. Their protection also depends on your device's security, such as your screen lock and encryption. Your tasks are never sent over the network. Data that the ads SDK sends to Google is encrypted in transit.

8. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, laws such as the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) or the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA) give you rights to access, correct, delete or port your personal data, to object to or restrict processing, and to withdraw consent. In this app:

  • Your tasks: they are shown in the app and you can edit or delete them at any time. We hold no copy.
  • Ad consent: change it in Settings → Privacy options (where shown) or limit ad personalization in your Android settings.
  • Advertising data held by Google: manage it through your Google account and the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Legal basis (EEA/UK): on-device storage of your tasks is needed to provide the app. Personalized advertising is based on your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.
  • Sale or sharing: we do not sell your tasks or personal information. Under some US state laws, sharing device data with Google for ads may count as "sharing" for targeted advertising; you can opt out by turning off ad personalization in your Android settings.

Because we receive no data from the app ourselves, we cannot look up or recover data stored on your device.

9. How to delete your data

NoirList has no account, so there is no account to delete. To delete your data:

  1. Delete one task: tap the × next to it.
  2. Delete finished tasks: tap Clear done → confirm Clear.
  3. Remove everything: uninstall NoirList, or use Settings → Apps → NoirList → Storage → Clear storage. This also removes your saved ad consent choice.
  4. Advertising ID: reset or delete it in your Android ad settings.

10. Children's privacy

NoirList is a general-audience app. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or under the age of digital consent where you live). If you believe a child has used the app and their data was collected for advertising, contact us and we will help.
