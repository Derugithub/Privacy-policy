1. Overview

CalSnap is a meal and calorie tracker. You create an account with your email address and a password, enter body details to calculate daily targets, and log meals from a photo, a nutrition label, a barcode, typed text or your voice. To estimate nutrition, CalSnap sends your meal photo, text or voice recording to our backend, and the backend uses a third-party AI service. Your account and logs are stored on our backend, hosted by Supabase, so that you can sign in and see your diary.

App identifier: Android package com.deredo.CalSnap.

Nutrition values are AI-generated or database estimates. They can be wrong, and they are not medical or dietary advice.

What we collect

  • Account: email address and password (the password is stored by our authentication provider in hashed form)
  • Profile and health/fitness details you enter: age, sex, height, current and target weight, activity level, goal, calculated targets
  • Meal logs, meal photos, nutrition-label photos, typed meal descriptions and voice recordings of meal descriptions
  • Weight logs, optional progress photos and water logs
  • Barcode numbers and food search terms (sent to Open Food Facts)

What we don't do

  • No ads, advertising IDs or tracking across apps
  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDKs
  • No selling of personal data
  • No location, contacts or health-app (HealthKit/Health Connect) access
  • No use of your data to build advertising profiles

5. AI processing of your photos, text and voice

When you analyze a meal, CalSnap sends the content you captured to an AI service:

  • Photos (meal or nutrition label) are compressed on your device, sent to our Supabase function, and forwarded to OpenAI's GPT-4o model, or to Google's Gemini model if that is the configured provider.
  • Typed descriptions are sent the same way.
  • Voice recordings are sent to OpenAI's Whisper API to be transcribed, and the transcript is then analyzed.

The AI service returns an estimated meal name, ingredients, calories and macronutrients. You can review and edit the estimate before saving. Nothing is saved to your diary until you confirm it. Avoid capturing people, documents or other unrelated personal information in meal photos or recordings.

The AI providers process this content under their own API terms. According to OpenAI's published API data-usage policy, API inputs are not used to train OpenAI models by default and may be retained for a limited period (currently up to 30 days) for abuse monitoring. If Google Gemini is used, Google's Gemini API terms apply, and on some unpaid service tiers Google may use inputs to improve its products. See the links in the Third-party services section.

By using a photo, text or voice analysis feature, you agree to this processing. If you do not want your content sent to an AI provider, you can log meals by barcode, from your recent and favorite foods, or by editing values yourself.

7. Legal bases (EEA/UK users)

We process your account and diary data to provide the service you asked for (performance of a contract). We process health and fitness data and AI analysis content based on your explicit consent, which you give by entering the data or using the analysis feature. You can withdraw it at any time by deleting the data or your account. We process limited technical data based on our legitimate interest in keeping the service secure.

8. Data retention

We keep your account and the data linked to it (profile, meals, meal photos, weight, water, favorites and progress photos) for as long as your account exists. When you delete a meal in the app, the meal, its items and its stored photo are deleted from our backend. When your account is deleted, we delete your account and all linked records and photos within 30 days. Copies in our hosting provider's routine backups are overwritten on its backup schedule.

AI providers and Open Food Facts keep request data only as described in their own policies (see above). Preferences and your session token stay on your device until you sign out or uninstall the app.

9. Data security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data:

  • All traffic between the app and our backend is encrypted with HTTPS/TLS.
  • Database row-level security policies limit each signed-in user to their own records.
  • Meal and progress photos are kept in private storage buckets and shown only through short-lived signed links (1 hour).
  • Your session token is kept in the iOS Keychain or Android Keystore.
  • AI provider API keys stay on the server and are not shipped in the app.

No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. Use a strong, unique password for your account.

10. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EEA/UK, or the CCPA/CPRA in California), you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you and receive a copy (portability).
  • Correct inaccurate data. You can edit your profile, targets and meals in the app.
  • Delete your data and your account (see the next section).
  • Withdraw consent at any time, for example by revoking camera, microphone or photo permissions, stopping AI analysis, or deleting your account. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that already happened.
  • Object to or restrict certain processing.
  • Not be discriminated against for exercising your rights.

We do not sell your personal information and do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. To exercise any right, email [MY_SUPPORT_EMAIL] from the email address on your account. We may need to verify your identity, and we will respond within 30 days (or the period required by law). You may also complain to your local data-protection authority.

11. How to delete your data and account

Delete your account and all associated data

  1. Send an email to [MY_SUPPORT_EMAIL] from the email address you use to sign in to CalSnap.
  2. Use the subject line "Delete my CalSnap account". You do not need to include your password.
  3. We may reply to confirm that the request came from you.
  4. Within 30 days we permanently delete your sign-in account, profile, body measurements and targets, all meal logs and meal items, favorites, weight and water logs, and all meal and progress photos stored on our backend. We will email you when deletion is complete.
  5. Then, on your phone, sign out (Profile → Sign out) and uninstall the app to remove the preferences stored on the device.

Delete individual items without deleting your account

  • A meal (and its photo): open the meal from your diary and delete it.
  • Weight entries, water logs or progress photos: email [MY_SUPPORT_EMAIL] from your account address saying which items to delete. We will remove them within 30 days.

Deleting the app alone does not delete your account or the data on our backend.

12. Children's privacy

CalSnap is a general-audience app. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or under the age of digital consent where you live). The app's profile requires an age of 13 or older. If you are under the age of digital consent where you live (up to 16 in some EEA countries), use CalSnap only with permission from a parent or guardian. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will help remove it.

13. Tracking and advertising

CalSnap does not track you. As Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework defines it, tracking means linking data collected in this app with data from other companies' apps, websites or offline properties for targeted advertising or advertising measurement, or sharing it with data brokers. CalSnap does none of that. It shows no ads, contains no advertising identifiers or analytics SDKs, and does not ask for tracking permission. Data sent to Supabase, OpenAI, Google or Open Food Facts is used only to provide the app's features. It is not linked with third-party data for advertising.
