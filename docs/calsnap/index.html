1. Overview

CalSnap is a meal and calorie tracker. You create an account with your email address and a password, enter body details to calculate daily targets, and log meals from a photo, a nutrition label, a barcode, typed text or your voice. To estimate nutrition, CalSnap sends your meal photo, text or voice recording to our backend, and the backend uses a third-party AI service. Your account and logs are stored on our backend, hosted by Supabase, so that you can sign in and see your diary.

App identifier: Android package com.deredo.CalSnap.

Nutrition values are AI-generated or database estimates. They can be wrong, and they are not medical or dietary advice.

What we collect

  • Account: email address and password (the password is stored by our authentication provider in hashed form)
  • Profile and health/fitness details you enter: age, sex, height, current and target weight, activity level, goal, calculated targets
  • Meal logs, meal photos, nutrition-label photos, typed meal descriptions and voice recordings of meal descriptions
  • Weight logs, optional progress photos and water logs
  • Barcode numbers and food search terms (sent to Open Food Facts)

What we don't do

  • No live advertising in the current closed-testing configuration; Android includes Google AdMob test ads
  • No separate analytics or crash-reporting SDKs; the advertising SDK may collect diagnostics and interaction data
  • No selling of personal data
  • No precise device-location, contacts or health-app (HealthKit/Health Connect) access; Google may estimate general location from your IP address
  • No sending your meal content, account details or health/fitness records to AdMob for advertising profiles

5. AI processing of your photos, text and voice

When you analyze a meal, CalSnap sends the content you captured to an AI service:

  • Photos (meal or nutrition label) are compressed on your device, sent to our Supabase function, and forwarded to Google's Gemini model.
  • Typed descriptions are sent the same way.
  • Voice recordings are sent the same way, and Gemini analyzes the audio directly.

The AI service returns an estimated meal name, ingredients, calories and macronutrients. You can review and edit the estimate before saving. Nothing is saved to your diary until you confirm it. Avoid capturing people, documents or other unrelated personal information in meal photos or recordings.

Google processes this content under the Gemini API terms. CalSnap uses Google's free (unpaid) Gemini API tier, and on unpaid tiers Google may use inputs and outputs to improve its products, which can include human review. See the links in the Third-party services section.

By using a photo, text or voice analysis feature, you agree to this processing. If you do not want your content sent to Google, you can log meals by barcode, from your recent and favorite foods, or by editing values yourself.

7. Legal bases (EEA/UK users)

We process your account and diary data to provide the service you asked for (performance of a contract). We process health and fitness data and AI analysis content based on your explicit consent, which you give by entering the data or using the analysis feature. You can withdraw it at any time by deleting the data or your account. We process limited technical data based on our legitimate interest in keeping the service secure.

Where required, Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) requests consent for advertising-related processing. Ad requests are enabled only when UMP reports that ads may be requested. You can review or change choices through Profile → Privacy options when Google requires that option. Consent to advertising is separate from consent to AI processing.

8. Data retention

We keep your account and the data linked to it (profile, meals, meal photos, weight, water, favorites and progress photos) for as long as your account exists. When you delete a meal in the app, the meal, its items and its stored photo are deleted from our backend. When your account is deleted, we delete your account and all linked records and photos within 30 days. Copies in our hosting provider's routine backups are overwritten on its backup schedule.

AI providers and Open Food Facts keep request data only as described in their own policies (see above). Preferences and your session token stay on your device until you sign out or uninstall the app.

Local ad counters and reward expiry remain in app storage until that storage is cleared or the app is uninstalled. Google manages advertising SDK data and consent records under its own policies; deleting your CalSnap account does not itself delete data held independently by Google.

9. Data security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data:

  • All traffic between the app and our backend is encrypted with HTTPS/TLS.
  • Database row-level security policies limit each signed-in user to their own records.
  • Meal and progress photos are kept in private storage buckets and shown only through short-lived signed links (1 hour).
  • Your session token is kept in the iOS Keychain or Android Keystore.
  • AI provider API keys stay on the server and are not shipped in the app.

No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. Use a strong, unique password for your account.

10. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EEA/UK, or the CCPA/CPRA in California), you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you and receive a copy (portability).
  • Correct inaccurate data. You can edit your profile, targets and meals in the app.
  • Delete your data and your account (see the next section).
  • Withdraw consent at any time, for example by revoking camera, microphone or photo permissions, stopping AI analysis, or deleting your account. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that already happened.
  • Object to or restrict certain processing.
  • Not be discriminated against for exercising your rights.

We do not sell your account, meal or health/fitness data. Google's advertising SDK processes technical data as described above. The current Android configuration uses test ads rather than live personalized advertising. To exercise any right, email movadmob@gmail.com from the email address on your account. We may need to verify your identity, and we will respond within 30 days (or the period required by law). You may also complain to your local data-protection authority.

11. How to delete your data and account

Delete your account and all associated data

  1. Send an email to movadmob@gmail.com from the email address you use to sign in to CalSnap.
  2. Use the subject line "Delete my CalSnap account". You do not need to include your password.
  3. We may reply to confirm that the request came from you.
  4. Within 30 days we permanently delete your sign-in account, profile, body measurements and targets, all meal logs and meal items, favorites, weight and water logs, and all meal and progress photos stored on our backend. We will email you when deletion is complete.
  5. Then, on your phone, sign out (Profile → Sign out) and uninstall the app to remove the preferences stored on the device.

Delete individual items without deleting your account

  • A meal (and its photo): open the meal from your diary and delete it.
  • Weight entries, water logs or progress photos: email movadmob@gmail.com from your account address saying which items to delete. We will remove them within 30 days.

Deleting the app alone does not delete your account or the data on our backend.

CalSnap is a general-audience app. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or under the age of digital consent where you live). The app's profile requires an age of 13 or older. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will help remove it.

13. Tracking and advertising

CalSnap's Android app integrates Google AdMob through the Google Mobile Ads SDK. The current closed-testing configuration uses Google's test application ID and test ad units, not live ad units. It supports banners on Home and Progress, occasional interstitials after meal logging, and an optional rewarded ad for a one-hour ad-free pass. Ad availability depends on consent status, frequency limits and loading conditions. Test ads do not mean the SDK is absent or that no technical data is transmitted.

According to Google's SDK disclosure, the SDK can collect and share IP addresses (which may indicate general location), app/ad interactions, diagnostics and device identifiers such as advertising ID and app set ID, subject to configuration, consent and device settings. Google uses SDK data for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. CalSnap does not pass meal photos, voice inputs, body measurements, diary contents or account email to AdMob, or use those records for ad targeting.

CalSnap checks Google's UMP consent information on launch, displays a consent form when required, and initializes the ad SDK and enables ad loads only when UMP reports that ads may be requested. When required, open Profile → Privacy options to review or change your choices. Android's advertising settings also let you reset or delete your advertising ID; menu names vary by device.

Apple defines tracking as linking app data with data from other companies' apps, websites or offline properties for targeted advertising or advertising measurement, or sharing with data brokers. The current ad implementation is Android-only; it does not enable iOS ads or an App Tracking Transparency request. We will review disclosures and applicable consent requirements before enabling live advertising or adding iOS advertising.
