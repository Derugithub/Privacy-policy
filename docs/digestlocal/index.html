1. Overview

DigestLocal is an offline reading shelf. You paste or share the link to a public web page, the app downloads that page and its images once, extracts the article, and saves it in a database and folder on your device. Reading, searching, listening with the system voice, summaries and quizzes all work on the saved copy, on the device. There is no account, no custom backend and no cloud sync.

The app is free and shows ads from Google AdMob, starting from your second session (never during your first session). In the EEA, UK and Switzerland, the app asks for consent on first launch through Google's consent form. Ads are requested only after that consent allows ad requests. Personalized ads are used only if you consent; if you decline, ads are limited or non-personalized. Outside those regions, ads follow Google's default settings for that region, and the consent form may not be shown. If the device is offline at launch, the app uses the previously saved consent choice. Your saved articles, links, summaries and quiz results are never sent to the ad network.

App identifier: Android package app.digestlocal.mobile.

What we collect

  • Links (URLs) you save or share into the app, and the article text, title and images downloaded from them, stored only on your device
  • Summaries and quizzes generated on the device, your theme choice, and simple ad counters (session count, saves, reward timers, quiz extras per article, quiz offer counts), stored only on your device
  • Google AdMob (from session 2) collects the advertising ID, approximate location derived from your IP address, app interaction and ad performance data, device and diagnostic information, and your consent status

What we don't do

  • No account or personal details
  • No sending of your saved articles, links, reading history, searches or quiz results to us or to the ad network
  • No personalized (targeted) ads in the EEA, UK or Switzerland unless you allow them in Google's consent form; if you decline, ads there are limited or non-personalized. Outside those regions, ads follow Google's default settings for that region. No cross-app tracking by the app itself
  • No AI or language-model service (summaries are computed on the device)
  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDK of our own
  • No payments, and no selling of your data

3. Third-party services and SDKs

The app is built with open-source libraries (Expo, React Native and related packages, including expo-share-intent for receiving shared links). These libraries run inside the app on your device and are not configured to send your data to us. The one third-party service that receives data is Google's advertising SDK.

Google AdMob and the User Messaging Platform (UMP): from your second session, the app uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (react-native-google-mobile-ads) to show a banner on the shelf, a banner at the end of each article, an interstitial ad after every third article you save (from your third session), and optional rewarded ads that you choose to watch (for example, to unlock extra quiz questions, 1.5×/2× listening speeds for 24 hours, or a 1-hour ad-free break, which can be extended up to 3 hours). The SDK is not started in your first session, and its measurement start-up is delayed until the app initializes it. Before ads load, Google's UMP SDK checks whether consent is needed. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland, the app asks for consent on first launch through Google's consent form. Ads load only when that consent status allows ad requests. Personalized ads are used only if you consent; if you decline, the app requests non-personalized (limited) ads. Outside those regions, ads follow Google's default settings for that region, and the consent form may not be shown. If the device is offline at launch, the app uses the previously saved consent choice. Ad requests are limited to general-audience content. The app does not pass article text, saved links, titles, keywords or page URLs to Google.

When it serves ads, Google may collect: the device advertising identifier (the Android advertising ID and app set ID), approximate location derived from your IP address, app interaction and ad performance data (such as ad impressions and taps), device and diagnostic information (such as device model, operating system and SDK performance), and your consent status. Google uses this data to deliver and measure ads, cap how often ads appear, and detect fraud, including when ads are limited or non-personalized. Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Text-to-speech: the Listen feature uses your operating system's built-in speech synthesizer (Google or your device maker). The article text is passed to that system service on the device and is not uploaded by the app. Websites you save: see "Network use" below.

5. Network use: fetching the pages and images you save

When you save a new link, the app sends a standard web request directly from your device to the website at that link. It then downloads the images in the article (up to 30) directly from the servers that host them, which can be different companies from the website itself (for example, image CDNs). Like any web request, these requests reveal your IP address and basic request details (a user agent identifying DigestLocal, accepted content types. Image requests send no referrer. If an image host refuses the request, the app retries once and sends only the article site's address (for example https://example.com), never the full page address) to those websites, image hosts and their hosting providers. They are third parties. Their own privacy policies apply, and they may log the request. The app does not run the page's scripts or store its cookies. Images are stored on your device and are not downloaded again when you read, search, listen or study. Saving a link that is already on your shelf does not contact the network.

Ad requests go from your device to Google, as described in section 3. Apart from these page, image and ad requests, the app makes no other network requests.

If you tap a link inside an article, it opens in your device's web browser, and the browser's and website's privacy policies apply.

6. Data retention

Saved articles, their images, summaries and quizzes stay on your device until you remove them from your shelf, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app. Ad counters and preferences stay on your device until you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.

Data collected by Google through the ads SDK is held by Google and retained under Google's own terms and policies (see the Google Privacy Policy). We do not receive it in a form that identifies you and cannot delete it for you.

Your device's operating system may include DigestLocal's data in device backups that you control (for example, Google device backup on Android). Those backups are handled by Google under your account and its privacy policy (Google Privacy Policy). We have no access to them.

7. Data security

Your data stays in the app's private storage area on your device, which the operating system protects from other apps. Its protection also depends on your device's security, such as your screen lock and device encryption. We recommend you keep your device locked and up to date. Your saved articles are never sent over the network by the app. Page and image downloads use the security of the websites you link to (HTTPS where the site supports it). Google states that data collected by its ads SDK is encrypted in transit.

8. Your rights and choices

Because DigestLocal keeps your library only on your device, you control it directly and we hold no copy of it. Depending on where you live, laws such as the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) or the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA) give you rights to access, correct, delete or port your personal data, to object to or restrict processing, and to withdraw consent. In this app:

  • Access and correction: the data the app stores is shown in the app, and you can edit it wherever the app allows editing.
  • Deletion: remove each article with Remove from shelf, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app.
  • Ad consent: in the EEA, UK and Switzerland, Google's consent form is shown on first launch, before ads are requested. You can change or withdraw your consent at any time in Settings → Privacy & ads → Privacy options (where that entry is shown). You can also uninstall the app, or reset the advertising ID in Android Settings → Google → Ads.
  • Advertising ID: on Android, go to Settings → Privacy → Ads (or Settings → Google → Ads) to reset or delete your advertising ID.
  • Rewarded ads are optional: you never have to watch one to use the app.
  • Withdrawing consent: you can revoke any device permission at any time in your device settings.
  • No sale: we do not sell your personal information. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland, personalized ads are used only if you consent; if you decline, ads are limited or non-personalized and are not requested for cross-context behavioral advertising. Outside those regions, ads follow Google's default settings for that region.

If you have a question about your rights, contact us at the address below. Because we receive no data from the app, we cannot look up or recover data stored on your device. For data held by Google, see Google's privacy controls.

9. How to delete your data

DigestLocal has no account, so there is no account to delete. To delete your data:

  1. Remove an article: open it → scroll to the end → Remove from shelf → Confirm remove. Its text, images, summary and quiz are deleted.
  2. Remove everything: uninstall DigestLocal. On Android you can also use Settings → Apps → DigestLocal → Storage → Clear storage. This also deletes the ad counters and preferences.
  3. Ad data: reset or delete your advertising ID as described in section 8. Data already collected by Google is handled under Google's terms.

10. Children's privacy

DigestLocal is not directed at children under 13 (or under the age of digital consent where you live), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. The app shows ads and is not part of any family or children's program. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will help remove it.

11. Tracking and advertising

DigestLocal shows ads from Google AdMob. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland, the app asks for consent on first launch through Google's consent form. Ads are requested only after that consent allows ad requests. Personalized ads are used only if you consent; if you decline, the app requests non-personalized (limited) ads. Outside those regions, ads follow Google's default settings for that region, and the consent form may not be shown. If the device is offline at launch, the app uses the previously saved consent choice. Tracking here means linking data collected in this app with data from other companies' apps, websites or offline properties for targeted advertising or advertising measurement, or sharing it with data brokers. DigestLocal does not implement its own cross-app tracking. You can change or withdraw consent in Settings → Privacy & ads → Privacy options where that entry is shown. Even when ads are limited or non-personalized, Google may still access the device advertising identifier (where your device settings allow it), your IP address and the other data listed in section 3 for ad delivery, frequency capping, aggregated measurement and fraud prevention. Your saved articles, links, searches and quiz results are never shared with Google or any ad network.
